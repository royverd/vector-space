N-Space

vector space & linear transform viewer

Dimensions

Axes 0–2 render as X/Y/Z. Axis 3+ are embedded on a spread of extra directions so they stay visually distinct.

Transform matrix (M)

Type new values, then click Jump or Animate to apply — editing alone doesn't move anything.
Animate eases out — fast at the start, slowing into the landing — rather than moving at constant speed.
Any matrix here is already linear: origin fixed, lines stay straight and parallel, no exceptions. Rigid mode adds the one extra property linearity alone doesn't give you — spacing and angles preserved — by orthonormalizing M (Gram–Schmidt) into a pure rotation/reflection before applying it. Off, M is used as typed: shear and non-uniform scale are allowed, so spacing can change.

Origin anchoring

Adds a translation after M·v — this makes the map affine, not linear, so the grid and vectors no longer stay pinned to the origin.

Vectors

Display

Translucent parallelogram between every pair of vectors, blended from their two colors — the plane their combination spans.

View offset

Drag on empty space to grab and offset the whole scene; drag directly on the grid, an axis, or a vector to rotate instead.
